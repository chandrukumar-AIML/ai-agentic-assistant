# ETHOS.md — Product Philosophy

> Why we built AI Agentic, what we believe, and how those beliefs shape every decision.

---

## Why We Built This

India has 63 million SMBs. Most of them pay a CA ₹5,000/month, a social media freelancer ₹8,000/month, and a WhatsApp support person ₹12,000/month — for work that AI can do in seconds.

But the AI tools that exist are:
- Built for American businesses (USD pricing, US tax law, English only)
- Fragmented (10 subscriptions for 10 jobs)
- Too complex for a textile trader in Surat or a CA firm in Chennai

We built AI Agentic because **a Tier-2 Indian business owner deserves the same AI leverage as a Silicon Valley startup** — in their language, for their rules, at a price they can actually pay.

---

## What We Believe

**1. One login. Every job.**
A business owner should not manage 10 AI subscriptions. One product, one login, all business AI.

**2. India-first, not India-adapted.**
GST, TDS, ITR, Companies Act, UPI, Tamil, Hindi — built in from day one. Not translated after the fact.

**3. Demo before you commit.**
Every feature works instantly in Demo Mode (zero cost, zero API key). Try before you trust.

**4. Depth over breadth.**
40 CA features that actually work > 200 features that half-work. We go deep on each vertical.

**5. AI that explains itself.**
Every output should be readable, actionable, and correct — not just impressive-sounding. If a CA can't verify it, we haven't done our job.

**6. Built for the person, not the enterprise.**
Priya Sharma running her CA practice from a laptop in Chennai is our user. Not a procurement committee.

---

## Design Principles

- **Dark by default** — serious tools for serious work look serious
- **Fast to first value** — user should see AI output within 3 clicks of login
- **No re-entering context** — workspace memory means the AI already knows your business
- **Honest about limits** — when AI can't help, say so clearly and point to a human expert

---

## What We Will Not Build

- Generic "AI assistant" chat boxes with no domain depth
- Features that require users to know how to prompt AI
- Anything that requires a 30-minute onboarding call
- Tools priced for enterprise while marketed to SMBs

---

## The Standard We Hold Ourselves To

Before shipping any feature, ask:
> "Would Priya Sharma (CA, Chennai, 12 clients, uses WhatsApp for everything) find this genuinely useful in her Monday morning workflow?"

If the answer is "maybe" — it's not ready.
If the answer is "yes, immediately" — ship it.
