# User Personas — AI Agentic Assistant

> These 5 personas were built from real Indian SMB pain points and guide every feature decision.

---

## Persona 1 — Rajesh Kumar (E-commerce Owner)

**Role:** Founder, ShopEasy (online fashion & lifestyle)
**Location:** Chennai, Tamil Nadu
**Team Size:** 8 people
**Revenue:** ₹25L/month

### Daily Pain Points
- 150+ WhatsApp customer messages per day (order status, returns, complaints)
- Team writes the same replies 30 times a day — wasting 2 hours
- No visibility on which products cause the most support tickets
- CSAT tracking done on a paper notebook

### What They Need
- **CS Agent:** Auto-draft WhatsApp replies, categorize tickets, CSAT surveys
- **Social Agent:** Daily Instagram + LinkedIn posts without hiring a content writer
- Language: Tamil + English mixed (Tanglish)

### Jobs to Be Done
```
When I get 50 WhatsApp complaints → I want one-click templated replies
When I run a sale → I want 7 days of social posts scheduled in 5 minutes
When I want to know my CSAT → I want a dashboard, not a spreadsheet
```

## Persona 2 — Priya Sharma (CA Partner)

**Role:** Partner, Sharma & Co (Chartered Accountants)
**Location:** Bangalore, Karnataka
**Team Size:** 12 (5 CAs, 7 staff)
**Clients:** 85 SMB clients

### Daily Pain Points
- Manually calculating GST for 85 clients every month
- Chasing clients for TDS certificates before deadlines
- Drafting the same client advisory emails with minor changes
- Missing compliance deadlines because no automated reminder system

### What They Need
- **CA Agent:** GST queries, TDS calculation, GSTR filing prep, compliance calendar
- **Email templates:** Professional CA advisory emails with correct tax figures
- Language: English (formal)

### Jobs to Be Done
```
When a client asks "what's GST on my service?" → answer in 10 seconds
When March 31 approaches → remind all clients what's due with exact amounts
When I need a client email → draft it with all tax figures pre-filled
```

## Persona 3 — Kavitha Nair (Agency Owner)

**Role:** Founder, KN Digital (social media & performance marketing)
**Location:** Kochi, Kerala
**Team Size:** 6
**Clients:** 20 SMB brands

### Daily Pain Points
- Creating 30+ posts per week across 20 client accounts
- Content ideas run out after 2 weeks
- Hashtag research takes 30 minutes per post
- Client reports take half a day to compile

### What They Need
- **Social Agent:** Content calendar, caption writing, hashtag research, competitor analysis
- **CS Agent:** Client communication templates, weekly performance summaries
- Language: English + Malayalam

### Jobs to Be Done
```
When I onboard a new client → generate 30-day content calendar in 5 minutes
When I need captions → give me 5 options with hooks and CTAs
When client asks for report → generate WhatsApp-ready performance summary
```

## Persona 4 — Dr. Arun Menon (Clinic Owner)

**Role:** Founder, MedPlus Clinic (general practice + diagnostics)
**Location:** Hyderabad, Telangana
**Team Size:** 4 (1 doctor, 3 staff)
**Patients:** 40/day

### Daily Pain Points
- Patient follow-up reminders done manually by calling each patient
- Insurance claim forms take 20 minutes each to fill
- No way to quickly look up drug interactions or symptom differentials
- Billing and GST on medical services confusing

### What They Need
- **CS Agent:** Patient follow-up sequences, appointment reminders
- **CA Agent:** GST on healthcare services, clinic billing
- Language: Telugu + English

### Jobs to Be Done
```
When a patient visits → generate follow-up reminder sequence for 7/30 days
When I need insurance claim → fill form from patient notes automatically
When a patient calls with symptoms → give me a structured triage checklist
```

## Persona 5 — Arjun Singh (Startup Founder)

**Role:** CEO, FreshMart (B2B grocery delivery to restaurants)
**Location:** Delhi NCR
**Team Size:** 25
**Revenue:** ₹1.2Cr/month

### Daily Pain Points
- Sales team has no consistent lead qualification process
- Customer complaints escalate to founders without a proper CS funnel
- No social media presence despite being B2B
- HR onboarding is ad-hoc — every new hire has a different experience

### What They Need
- **CS Agent:** Escalation rules, SLA tracking, agent scorecards
- **CA Agent:** Business valuation, advance tax, MCA/ROC compliance
- **Social Agent:** LinkedIn B2B content for restaurant decision-makers
- Language: Hindi + English

### Jobs to Be Done
```
When a high-value customer complains → escalate to manager in < 2hrs automatically
When I hire someone → onboarding plan ready in minutes
When I need to raise funding → business valuation + pitch deck support
```
